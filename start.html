Starting Your Experience

Start Your Project on the Right Foot

The beginning of an engaged learning experience is where your preparation turns into a working partnership. Use this stage to establish relationships, clarify roles and responsibilities, align expectations, and create communication practices that will guide your team throughout the project.

Why a Strong Start Matters

First impressions can shape the direction of a partnership. Early conversations give your team and partner an opportunity to clarify what you are trying to accomplish, how you will work together, and what each person can expect from the experience.

Taking time to establish expectations early can reduce confusion later. A strong project launch creates shared understanding, builds trust, and gives everyone a clearer way to respond when priorities or circumstances change.

What to Focus on When You Start

Establish Partner Communication

Make your first contact professional and welcoming. Introduce your team, clarify the purpose of the project, and establish how and when you will communicate with your partner.

Plan Your First Meeting

Use your kickoff meeting to learn more about your partner, confirm project goals, ask questions, discuss constraints, and identify what a successful collaboration might look like.

Define Roles and Responsibilities

Make sure team members and partners understand who is responsible for different parts of the project. Discuss decision-making, meeting responsibilities, deadlines, and points of contact.

Agree on How You Will Work Together

Establish expectations for communication, meetings, response times, project updates, and shared documents. Record these agreements so your team can return to them throughout the experience.

Questions to Answer Before Moving Forward

  • What does our partner hope this project will accomplish?
  • What does success look like for our team and our partner?
  • Who is responsible for each part of the project?
  • How often will we meet or provide updates?
  • Which communication channels will we use?
  • How will decisions and changes to the project be documented?
  • What should we do if expectations or priorities change?

Starting Your Experience Checklist

  1. Send an introductory message to your partner.
  2. Prepare an agenda for your kickoff meeting.
  3. Confirm project goals and expected outcomes.
  4. Define team and partner roles.
  5. Agree on communication methods and meeting schedules.
  6. Document the project scope and responsibilities.
  7. Identify your next steps and immediate deadlines.
